## Decisions that block controlled corrections

The owner can approve field-level factual corrections under the proposed workflow without resolving every future UX option. Before a production correction, approve its exact value, product scope, provenance, and rights disposition. Before changing recommendation eligibility, explicitly decide items 3–7 and test rollback. Root's age must not be described as verified for under-14 matches until its discrepancy is resolved; any interim treatment requires explicit owner approval.

The three-game test is suitable for a **controlled next correction proposal**, not for publishing unreviewed changes: Rhino Hero's current listing is promising but legacy continuity is unproven; Codenames' current edition is distinct from the 2015 record; and Root's age conflict remains open. Next, have the owner review one field packet per game, capture actual decisions, then run one small follow-up sample to measure research effort and unresolved-field rate before scaling.

## Proposed Checkpoint 05B4 scope (not implemented)

1. Add a small, additive reviewed-facts layer keyed to stable GameFinder catalog identity plus explicit product/edition scope. Keep legacy BGG IDs and all Vault/history/score keys unchanged; no mass ID assignment.
2. Store only field value, units, scope, source reference, factual status (`verified`, `provisional`, `unknown`, `disputed`), owner decision, and separate rights status. Legacy values stay explicitly legacy/unverified.
3. If owner-reviewed evidence establishes the applicable Root recommendation, prepare one targeted age correction. Otherwise stage a reversible Root-only under-14 treatment for owner decision; do not make a catalog-wide status migration.
4. Test Root for age bands below and above its approved threshold, and verify unaffected games retain their existing behavior.
5. Add regression tests proving the additive facts layer does not rewrite, orphan, or reassign Vault, play-history, or score references.
6. Compare representative before/after result counts and candidate lists, including age bands 8–10, 11–13, and 14–17, with uncertainty and eligibility status visible.
7. Roll back by disabling/removing the reviewed-facts overlay and restoring the captured prior field/eligibility decision. Preserve the evidence and decision log; never reverse-map or merge user references automatically.
