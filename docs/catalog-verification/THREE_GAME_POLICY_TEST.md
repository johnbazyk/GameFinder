It is not approved for the legacy record.

**Best-supported interpretation:** there is a material, unresolved publisher-age conflict, and the FAQ confirms that a Root copy has a printing/edition number identifiable on its box and that player-board/marker content changed beginning with the third printing. This does not establish that the age recommendation changed, nor does it provide continuity evidence for age 10 or 14+ across printings or markets. Complexity is not an explanation for the age difference, and the community value 13 is a separate, unsourced claim. The FAQ provides a practical continuity path: identify the owned/reference copy's printing and edition from the box barcode, then compare edition/market-specific publisher evidence or request confirmation. Do not require a unique printing if the publisher establishes field-specific continuity; until then the age field remains disputed for the legacy row.

The legacy age 10 currently permits Root for ages 11–13 under existing age bands. This is a priority risk for confirmed recommendations, but this checkpoint does not change eligibility. During 05B4, measure Root's inclusion in those scenarios and present any temporary restriction/uncertainty behavior to the owner before implementation. Do not classify Root as adult content from an age recommendation.
