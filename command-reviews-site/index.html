Checking out a security company — including us

How long has Command Corporation been in business?

Where is Command Corporation located, and where do you work?
Our main office is in East Granby, Connecticut, with a second office in New Hampton, New Hampshire. We serve homes and businesses across Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New Hampshire.
What services do you actually provide?
Residential and commercial security systems, 24/7 alarm monitoring, fire alarm systems with monitoring, inspection and maintenance, high-resolution surveillance cameras, access control, intrusion detection, live video monitoring with visual verification, and locksmith services in New Hampshire.
Who monitors my alarm, and is it really 24/7?
Yes — alarm and fire signals are monitored around the clock, every day of the year. Do you serve both homes and businesses?
Yes. We design, install, and monitor systems for single homes up through multi-site commercial customers, including fire systems that carry inspection and code requirements.
What happens when my alarm goes off?
The signal reaches the monitoring center immediately, an operator follows the verification and contact procedure on your account, and dispatch is requested when the event is confirmed or can't be ruled out. When we install your system we walk you through exactly what that sequence looks like for your account — and you can test it.
What should I ask any security company before signing?
  • Show me your state license number, and let me verify it on the state's lookup site.
  • Who owns the monitoring center, and is it UL-listed?
  • What is the contract length, and what does cancellation cost?
  • Do I own the equipment when the contract ends?
  • What's your measured service response time, and over what period was it measured?
  • Can I speak to a customer who has been with you for five or more years?
Any established company can answer all six without flinching. We can.
How do I verify a security company's license in Connecticut?
Use the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection's official license lookup (linked in the "Verify us yourself" section above). Search by the company's name and check that the license is active, matches the exact legal name on your contract, and covers the work being proposed. New Hampshire and Massachusetts have their own lookups — check the state where the work will be done.
Will you pressure me with a door-to-door style sales pitch?
No. We visit your home or business, make recommendations, and give you a quote. Then it's your decision. A legitimate security company does not need same-day-only pricing or scare tactics — treat both as a reason to keep shopping.
